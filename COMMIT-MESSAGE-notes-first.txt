The notes come before the button that sends them

Six contracts in a row went out with notes null and item_notes empty, each one
created and sent inside ten seconds. Nothing was broken. The column, the RPC,
the payload and the renderer all worked, and every one of those agents would
have told you the deal had terms worth writing down.

The notes form sat two sections below the send button, and the send button is
the first control an agent's thumb reaches when they land on this page from the
quote. The order of the page was the bug.

WHAT CHANGED

ContractEditor is new and owns both blocks, so they can share one piece of
state. While the contract is a draft the notes come first and the actions
second. Once it is signed or cancelled there is nothing left to fill in, so the
actions lead and the notes read as a record of what was agreed.

ContractNotes reports two things upward: whether the form is holding text
nobody has saved, and whether the contract carries any saved notes at all. It
is the only place that knows either, so both are reported rather than guessed
at from props one level up.

SENDING IS REFUSED OVER UNSAVED TEXT

While the form is dirty the send button is disabled and says why. Text in a
textarea is not in the document, and the document is the only thing the
customer ever sees. The same state hangs a beforeunload guard on the tab,
because closing it is the commonest way to lose a note somebody typed.

SENDING WITH NO NOTES ASKS ONCE

Not a block. Plenty of deals carry nothing beyond the terms and the prices, and
an agent who means that should be able to say so in one extra click. The
question is asked once, only for a contract with nothing saved on it, and it
withdraws itself the moment notes exist: an agent who scrolls up, writes one and
saves it has answered it, and a banner still reading "no notes were added" over
a contract that now has them would be the page lying about what it is holding.

THE QUOTE'S NOTE IS OFFERED, NEVER COPIED

getQuoteNotes reads it and the form shows it beside the field with a button that
appends it. The quote note is where an agent writes everything a deal needs —
the arithmetic they did for themselves as much as what was promised to the
customer — and only they know which half belongs in a document somebody signs.
Copying it in silently would put the other half in front of the customer.
