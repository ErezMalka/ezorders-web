Give the agents a door they can find

The portal had no way in. /he/agent existed and worked, and the only route to it
was knowing the URL and typing it — which meant a bookmark on one machine, and a
question to somebody else every time an agent switched laptop or a new one was
hired.

So the footer carries a link now, last in the bottom bar after the copyright, in
the same muted grey as everything else there. Both locales point at /he/agent,
because the portal is Hebrew-only and an English-speaking agent still needs the
same door.

Deliberately plain. It is a staff entrance on a customer-facing page: findable by
the handful of people who need it, and uninteresting to everyone else. The login
behind it is Supabase Auth, and a visitor who does wander in and guesses an
address learns nothing — a failed sign-in is the same answer whether or not the
account exists.
